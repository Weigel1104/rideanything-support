隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 19 日（1.1 版起）

《万物冲下坡》（以下简称「本游戏」）是一款单机游戏，不需要注册，不收集你的姓名、联系方式等个人信息。为了免费提供游戏并把它做得更好玩，我们使用 Google AdMob 展示广告，并使用 Google 的 Firebase 统计游戏数据和收集崩溃报告。它们会收集下面列出的数据。

本游戏自己

  • 不需要注册或登录，不要求相机、麦克风、定位、通讯录等权限。
  • 游戏进度（最远距离、金币、已解锁座驾、升级、设置）保存在你的设备上；如果你登录了 iCloud，还会通过 Apple 的 iCloud 同步到你自己的其他设备，换手机也不会丢。这份存档只在你的 iCloud 账户里，我们看不到。
  • 如果你登录了 Game Center，你的最远距离和每日挑战成绩会提交到 Apple 的 Game Center 排行榜，并与你的 Game Center 昵称一起显示给其他玩家。这部分由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理。
  • App 内购买由 Apple 处理，本游戏拿不到你的姓名、付款方式或任何支付信息，只会收到「某件商品已购买」的确认。

游戏统计与崩溃报告（Google Analytics for Firebase、Firebase Crashlytics）

  • 用来了解游戏哪里太难、哪里不好玩，以及修复闪退：每局跑了多远、怎么结束的、骑了哪种坐骑，看了哪些奖励广告、买了什么商品，以及设备型号、系统版本、语言、由 IP 地址推算的大致地区（国家或城市级别）、随机生成的应用实例 ID。闪退时会上传崩溃记录。
  • 这些数据不包含你的姓名或联系方式。只有在你于系统弹窗中允许跟踪后，才可能与设备的广告标识符关联。
  • 统计数据保留 14 个月后自动删除。Google 如何处理这些数据：https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

广告（Google AdMob）

  • Google 可能收集：设备的广告标识符（仅在你于系统弹窗中允许跟踪后）、IP 地址推算的大致位置、广告展示与点击等使用数据、崩溃与性能数据，用于展示和衡量广告。
  • 你可以在游戏的「设置 → 广告隐私选项」（在法律要求的地区显示）修改同意；也可以随时在 iPhone「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」中关闭跟踪。拒绝不会影响游戏的任何内容。
  • Google 如何使用这些数据：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • 购买「去广告」后，本游戏不再展示插屏广告；你主动选择观看的奖励广告仍会使用上述服务。

本游戏不面向 13 岁以下儿童。数据处理方式如有变化，我们会先更新本页面再发布新版本。

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026 (version 1.1 onwards)

Ride Anything: Downhill Rush ("the game") is a single-player game. There is no sign-up, and the game does not collect your name, contact details or other personal information. To keep the game free and make it better we use Google AdMob to show ads, and Google's Firebase for game statistics and crash reports. They collect the data listed below.

The game itself

  • No account or sign-in, and no camera, microphone, location or contacts permissions.
  • Your progress (best distance, coins, unlocked rides, upgrades, settings) is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced through Apple's iCloud to your own other devices so a new phone keeps it. That copy lives only in your iCloud account; we cannot see it.
  • If you are signed in to Game Center, your best distance and daily challenge results are posted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards and shown to other players with your Game Center nickname. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy.
  • In-App Purchases are processed by Apple. The game never sees your name, payment method or payment details, only a confirmation that an item was bought.

Game statistics and crash reports (Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics)

  • Used to find out where the game is too hard or not fun, and to fix crashes: how far each run went, how it ended and which ride you used, which reward ads you watched and what you bought, plus device model, OS version, language, approximate region derived from your IP address (country or city level) and a randomly generated app instance ID. If the game crashes, a crash report is uploaded.
  • None of this includes your name or contact details. It can only be linked to your device's advertising identifier if you allow tracking in the system prompt.
  • Statistics are deleted automatically after 14 months. How Google handles this data: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Google may collect: your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking in the system prompt), approximate location derived from your IP address, ad usage data such as impressions and taps, and crash and performance data, to show and measure ads.
  • You can change your consent in the game under Settings → Ad privacy options (shown where the law requires it), and turn tracking off at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Saying no changes nothing in the game.
  • How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • With Remove Ads purchased, the game shows no interstitial ads; reward ads you choose to watch still use the service above.

The game is not directed at children under 13. If our data practices change, this page will be updated before the new version is released.
