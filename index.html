换手机后进度还在吗？

在新手机上登录同一个 iCloud 账户，打开游戏后进度会自动同步过来。

买过「去广告」或「新手礼包」，换手机后怎么找回？

在游戏的设置或商店里点「恢复购买」。金币包是消耗型商品，用掉后不能恢复。

排行榜里看不到自己的成绩？

需要先在 iPhone「设置 → Game Center」登录。登录后再跑一局，成绩就会提交。

手机发热或卡顿？

在游戏「设置 → 画质」选低一档。游戏也会在手机过热或开启低电量模式时自动降低画质。

不想被广告跟踪？

可以在 iPhone「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪」关闭，或在游戏「设置 → 广告隐私选项」修改（在法律要求的地区显示）。

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Yes. Sign in to the same iCloud account on the new phone and open the game; your progress syncs over.

How do I get Remove Ads or the Starter Pack back on a new phone?

Tap Restore Purchases in the game's Settings or Shop. Coin packs are consumable and can't be restored once spent.

My score isn't on the leaderboard

Sign in to Game Center first in iPhone Settings → Game Center, then play another run.

The phone gets hot or the game stutters

Pick a lower setting under Settings → Graphics in the game. The game also lowers the graphics by itself when the phone runs hot or Low Power Mode is on.

I don't want ad tracking

Turn it off in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or change your choice in the game under Settings → Ad privacy options (shown where the law requires it).
